Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stock Base ("Stock Base", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Stock Base mobile application (the "App").

Stock Base is an inventory management application designed to help users manage products, stock quantities, prices, categories and related inventory information.

Important: Stock Base is designed to keep your inventory information on your device unless a feature of the App specifically states otherwise. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide and improve Stock Base.

1.1 Information You Enter Into the App

When you use Stock Base, you may voluntarily enter information such as:

  • Product names
  • Product descriptions
  • Stock quantities
  • Prices and costs
  • Categories
  • Product codes or SKU information
  • Supplier or other inventory-related information
  • Notes and other information you choose to enter

This information is used to provide the inventory-management features of the App.

Unless a feature specifically states otherwise, inventory information entered into Stock Base is stored locally on your device and is not routinely transmitted to our servers.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

Stock Base itself is not designed to collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, contacts, or precise location simply because you use the App.

However, third-party services used by the App, including advertising services, may automatically process certain technical and advertising-related information.

Depending on the device, operating system, advertising configuration, consent choices and applicable law, this may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Information about interaction with advertisements
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Other information necessary to provide, measure or prevent fraud in advertising

2. Advertising

Stock Base is supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App, including banner, interstitial and/or rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob is a third-party advertising service operated by Google. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information from your device in order to provide, personalize, measure and protect advertising services, subject to your consent and applicable laws.

Advertising-related information may include device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, information about advertisements shown to you, interactions with advertisements, and other technical or diagnostic information.

The exact information processed can depend on your device, operating system, your consent choices, Google's policies, and the applicable advertising configuration.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

10. Local Storage of Inventory Data

Stock Base is designed to provide inventory-management functionality primarily through local storage on your device.

Information you enter into the App may therefore remain on your device and may be accessible to anyone who has access to your unlocked device or its stored data.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for your device and for keeping backups of important inventory information where necessary.

11. Data Retention

Inventory information stored locally by Stock Base generally remains on your device until you delete or modify it, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove the relevant application data.

Information processed by third-party services, such as advertising providers, may be retained according to those providers' policies, technical requirements, legal obligations, and applicable retention periods.

12. Data Deletion

Because inventory information may be stored locally on your device, you can generally remove that information using the deletion functionality provided within the App or by clearing the App's stored data or uninstalling the App.

Please note that deleting locally stored application data does not necessarily delete information that may already have been processed by independent third-party service providers such as advertising providers.

Where applicable law provides you with additional rights concerning personal information processed by us, you may contact us using the contact information below.
