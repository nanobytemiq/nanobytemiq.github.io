This Privacy Policy describes how Link Space ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Link Space mobile application.

Certain information may be collected automatically when you use the App, particularly through third-party services used by the App.

This information may include:

  • IP address

  • Information about interactions with advertisements
  • App launch and usage information
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information relating to crashes or errors
  • Other information required for advertising, security, and fraud prevention

Advertising providers may automatically collect certain information from your device in order to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertisements and to help prevent fraud and abuse.

  • IP address
